About the three PWS stations

All three Wunderground stations read warmer than the official KNYC METAR. The nowcast uses each one differently:

  • KNYNEWYO1796 — densest (5-min) and cleanest. Sits a steady ~+1.9°F above METAR, roughly flat all day. Best single station; its value is trustworthy after the fixed offset.
  • KNYNEWYO1686 — also clean, ~+2°F warm. Sharpest near midday (~+1°F), drifts warmer overnight (~+2.3°F). Trust its value, especially midday.
  • KNYNEWYO270 — solar-loaded: ~+2.8°F at midday vs ~+0.7°F at night, spiking past +11°F in strong sun. Also reports only every ~15 min. Trust its hourly change, not its level.

From the Aug 2024–Aug 2026 backfill (“Wunderground vs NWS — KNYC” notebook). Blend of all three beats any single station.
